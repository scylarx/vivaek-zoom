## About — Odissi Dance Company

> Nirmal Jena teaches his distinct family lineage of Odissi Indian classical dance and music from studios in Sydney city / eastern suburbs and the Blue Mountains.

> Nirmal and Chitrita are passionate about creativity, diversity, humanity and sustainability.

After establishing the Odissi Dance Company in Sydney, Nirmal and Chitrita Mukerjee founded **Arts and Life Education Gurukul Ltd (ALEG)**, an Australian charity with DGR status. The flagship program *Odissi: Dance for Humanity* provides "free and comprehensive training in Odissi dance and music, as well as life skills, to young people faced with financial hardship."

### About Odissi (existing framing)

Odissi developed in Odisha, an eastern Indian state along the Bay of Bengal. Its technique centres on the **chawk** (square position) and **tribhanga** (triangle / triple-bend), preserved in temple architecture throughout the region. The Jena style is "known for its authenticity and for powerfully integrating the body, mind and soul in the training process."

Odissi can be learned as daily practice, for performance, for teaching, or for combined goals.

### The Jena style (as described on the existing site)

Characteristics emphasised:
- Deep bends in basic positions.
- Undulating body movements through continuous level changes.
- Solo performance emphasis over dance-dramas.
- Exploration of the *raudra* (anger) and *bibatsa* (disgust) sentiments.
