# Caldera Product Brief

Status: working brief

## Core Intent

Caldera is a Sydney niche music events site built around community, trust, discovery, and "good people" energy. It should help promoters, artists, regulars, new people, and neurodivergent community members find a safe base to connect.

The site should feel alive, slightly strange, beautiful, and socially warm. It should not feel like a generic nightclub page, crypto landing page, or overdesigned festival poster.

## Audience

- People who already know the scene and want the next event quickly.
- Newcomers who need social confidence and safety signals.
- Neurodivergent people who want enough detail to understand the environment before arriving.
- Promoters and artists who want the brand to feel credible.
- Friends sharing a link in group chats.

## Experience Principles

- Mobile-first, desktop-beautiful.
- High sensory beauty, controlled sensory load.
- Dark base with purple, blue, and green accents, but not a one-note neon wash.
- Motion should feel responsive and organic, with a strong reduced-motion mode.
- Community and safety signals should be built into the experience, not hidden in a policy page.
- Audio should be opt-in, persistent, and respectful.

## Likely One-Page Structure

1. Hero: current/next event, community signal, visual identity, optional audio entry.
2. Events: upcoming, past, RSVP/ticket links, venue clarity.
3. Sound: featured tracks/playlists/mixes with attribution and opt-in player.
4. People: promoters, residents, artists, collaborators, community photos.
5. Safe Base: accessibility, neurodivergent-friendly details, transport, arrival expectations, code of care.
6. Contact: promoter submissions, volunteer/community contact, social links.

## Visual System Notes

- Use a 3D mandala as a living identity object, probably hero-adjacent or as a transitional anchor rather than a decorative footer afterthought.
- Build with Three.js/react-three-fiber and verify canvas rendering on desktop and mobile.
- Avoid making every section a card.
- Use neon edges, glows, and active states sparingly.
- Use real event/media assets as soon as available.

## Audio Product Notes

The player is a trust surface. It must show:

- what is playing;
- who made it;
- where it comes from;
- whether it is a preview, embed, or licensed local file;
- play/pause/skip;
- progress;
- external platform link.

No surprise audio on load.

## Content Needed

- Event names, dates, venues, ticket URLs.
- Instagram account(s) and source posts.
- Approved photos/videos.
- Artists/promoters list.
- Music links.
- Safety/code-of-care language.
- Accessibility and venue notes.

## Open Product Questions

- Is Caldera a recurring event brand, umbrella community, or event discovery platform?
- Is there one promoter or many?
- What is the desired level of public visibility versus semi-private community feel?
- Should the site collect emails/SMS/Discord interest?
- What is the first launch event or launch story?
